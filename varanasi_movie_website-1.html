The story

A myth written across time.

Varanasi is an upcoming Indian action-adventure epic directed by S. S. Rajamouli and starring Mahesh Babu, scheduled for a worldwide IMAX release on April 7, 2027.

The story follows Rudhra (Mahesh Babu), a rugged archaeologist and explorer who is recruited for a high-stakes mission when the ancient city of Varanasi is threatened by the impending impact of a massive asteroid named Shambhavi. To avert the disaster, Rudhra must travel the globe to recover a hidden cosmic artifact tied directly to the city's mythical past.

He is joined on his quest by Mandakini (Priyanka Chopra Jonas), a fierce historian and skilled marksman. As they piece together centuries-old secrets, they uncover that the asteroid is not a natural astronomical threat, but rather a calculated, targeted strike orchestrated by Kumbha (Prithviraj Sukumaran), a tech-genius mastermind intent on absolute global domination.

SS Rajamouli weaves Indian mythology directly into the sci-fi adventure, with a narrative that spans multiple eras—from the ancient Treta Yuga to the modern Kali Yuga.

“One city. Multiple eras. A threat from beyond the sky.”
